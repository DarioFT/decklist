Getting Started

Welcome to decklist.xyz! This free tool helps you generate professional tournament registration sheets for Magic: The Gathering events in seconds.

Quick Start Guide

  1. Enter your information: Fill in your name and DCI number (optional)
  2. Add event details: Event name, date, and location
  3. Paste your deck: Copy your decklist from MTGO, MTG Goldfish, or Tappedout and paste it directly
  4. Choose format: Select WotC (Wizards of the Coast) or SCG (Star City Games) sheet format
  5. Download: Click "Download PDF" to get your tournament-ready registration sheet

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the MTG decklist generator?

Simply paste your deck from MTGO, MTG Goldfish, Tappedout, or any supported format into the "Main Deck" field. The generator will automatically parse your cards, validate them, and create a tournament-ready PDF registration sheet for WotC or SCG events.

What deck formats are supported?

Our decklist generator supports MTGO (Magic Online), MTG Goldfish, Tappedout, MTG Salvation, Deckbox "Print" format, and MWS (Magic Workstation) formats. You can paste decklists directly without needing to separate the sideboard.

Can I create both WotC and SCG registration sheets?

Yes! You can choose between Wizards of the Coast (WotC) official tournament registration sheets or Star City Games (SCG) format sheets using the decksheet format selector.

How do I save and share my decklist?

Use the "Copy Permalink" button to generate a unique URL that pre-fills all your deck information. You can share this link with others or use it to quickly load your deck later. You can also save decklists locally using the "Save Decklist" button.

Does the generator validate card legality?

Yes! The generator automatically validates your cards against our database and will warn you if your deck contains fewer or more than 60 cards in the main deck.

Can I customize the logo on my decklist?

Absolutely! You can choose from various MTG logos or upload your own custom logo (max 180x72 pixels, JPEG or PNG format) to personalize your tournament registration sheet.

Is this decklist generator free to use?

Yes, decklist.xyz is completely free to use. Generate unlimited tournament registration sheets for Magic: The Gathering events without any cost or registration required.

Do I need to create an account?

No! decklist.xyz works entirely in your browser without any registration. Your deck information never leaves your computer unless you choose to share a permalink.

Supported Deck Formats

MTGO (Magic Online)

Export your deck from Magic Online and paste it directly. The format looks like:

4 Lightning Bolt
4 Counterspell
20 Island

Sideboard
3 Negate
2 Dispel

MTG Goldfish

Copy your deck from MTGGoldfish using the "Export" → "Text" option. Both main deck and sideboard will be parsed automatically.

Tappedout

Use Tappedout's "Text" export format. Click on your deck, then select the "Text" view and copy everything.

Manual Entry

You can also manually type your deck in this format:

Quantity CardName
4 Lightning Bolt
3 Snapcaster Mage
